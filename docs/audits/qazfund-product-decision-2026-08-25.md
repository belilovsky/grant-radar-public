# QAZ.FUND product decision — 2026-08-25

## Product role

QAZ.FUND is a public-interest navigator for grants and support programmes. Its
dominant job is to help a Kazakhstan applicant find an opportunity, verify it
at the official source, compare alternatives, prepare a browser-only working
draft, and export or hand off the result.

The primary users are individuals, teams, companies, NGOs, researchers and
other applicant organisations in Kazakhstan. Experts, editors, consultants,
public servants and programme operators are secondary evidence and hand-off
users.

## Primary path

`find -> verify -> compare -> prepare -> export/share`

The catalog and opportunity source remain the entry points. Comparison is a
selection state owned by the catalog. Preparation is local to the browser.
CSV, calendar and share actions transfer the user's result without creating a
server profile. Daily digest, QPost and technical APIs remain compatible
secondary draft/feed interfaces and are not the product's first-screen promise.

## Boundaries

QAZ.FUND is not a grant maker, eligibility authority, legal adviser or
application-submission portal. It does not add accounts, server-side applicant
profiles, document uploads, personal notifications or submission APIs in this
cycle. Relevance supports ordering and shortlisting; only the programme owner
can confirm eligibility, current conditions and receipt of an application.

## Decisions implemented

1. Added one compact semantic five-step journey to the catalog instead of a
   feature catalogue or technical-product pitch.
2. Restored catalog-owned compare selection and grouped the existing CSV, ICS
   and share outcomes without adding persistence or a new backend contract.
3. Replaced eligibility-sounding workflow and relevance language in RU, KK and
   EN with shortlist and evidence language.
4. Preserved the current teal visual character and SSR AVDS adapter; visual
   work was limited to confirmed route, contrast and responsive defects.

## Sustainability and governance

The public navigator remains useful without accounts or paid submission flows.
Its durable asset is a reproducible, source-attributed opportunity dataset and
an honest preparation/export workflow. Programme operators own eligibility and
application decisions; QAZ.FUND owns ingestion quality, public projection,
interface clarity and release evidence.
